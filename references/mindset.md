# The mindset: who is speaking, what they can see, and how it moves

The ledger says where meaning goes. Rhythm says what the reader feels.
Purpose says what the reader is owed. The mindset says who is speaking and
what they can see, and it sits upstream of all three.

## A state is a prior over readings

Every sentence holds a cloud of readings. The writer's state decides which
of them are open to the writer at all. A writer with one thing on their
mind cannot see a harbour as a harbour: every detail is a sign of that
thing. A furious writer sees one thing and nothing beside it. A tender one
sees the small thing nobody else would notice and names it. The manifold
the reader gets is already biased before a word is chosen, and the bias is
the state.

## It reaches the page through attention

The state picks the details. The anxious notice the exits; the drunk, the
floor; the insistent, the same thing three times. The details set the
length of the sentence (a mind that sees one thing writes short), the
length sets the rhythm, the rhythm sets what the reader feels. That is the
mechanism behind
"show, don't tell": you do not say the narrator is afraid, you let fear
choose what gets looked at.

## Felt and shown

The writer has a state and presents a state. The gap is voice control.
A furious person who writes a cold note at 1 is more frightening than one
who writes at 5, because the reader senses a reading kept closed on
purpose and carries the withheld anger as debt. Shown below felt makes
tension. Shown above felt is melodrama, the unearned punch. So the dial
has two values, and the difference between them is a signal the reader
reads. When the writer gives one number, it is the shown one.

## The trajectory

A fixed state is a setting. A shifting one is the story. The state follows
the ledger: a promise paid brings relief, one raised brings dread, a
reading closed brings calm, one opened against the writer's will brings
fear. So the state turns because of what was just paid, or against it
(therefore, or but), and a turn with no cause is the seam failure in
another costume. A state that never moves across a page is the hum in the
voice. Switch rhythm where the state turns, and nowhere else: a rhythm
change with no state change is a tic, a state change with no rhythm change
is told rather than shown.

The forms are state trajectories. The musical sentence is a state
accelerating. The villanelle is one state the same words pass through as
it changes. The rondo is a state that keeps returning home. Recapitulation
is the state the piece opened in, met from the other side. Felt and shown
can cross mid-page: the cold note that cracks in the last paragraph is
restraint failing, and that is a punch; the 5 that goes quiet is the
release. Mid-sentence, the only place a state may break is the dash as a
rest, where the sentence stops before the thing arrives.

## States by degree

Tendencies, not words. Each state at 1 is present and contained; at 5 it
has taken the page. The columns are what the state does to attention
(what is named, what is walked around), to the sentence, to the accents it
licenses, and to the tempos it leaves possible.

The state is not the reader's feeling. Rhythm carries the reader's; the
mindset is the speaker's, which the voice follows. In first person the
speaker and the character are one; in close third the narrator borrows the
character's state; in a message the speaker is the sender. The gaps are
where the effects live: dramatic irony is the reader opening a reading the
speaker's state cannot; an unreliable narrator is mindset at 5 with the
plain reading quietly compromised.

## Reading the state from a page

The analyze mode reads the state the page presents, never the state the
person is in. It is a reading of the text, not a diagnosis, and it is
offered as readings are: here is what the page does, here is what it
could do instead.

Signals: what is named and what is walked around; where sentences break
and how long they run; hedges (anxiety), intensifiers and negations
(insistence, defensiveness), second person (accusation), repetition
(obsession, or drink), questions (uncertainty, or the rhetorical setup),
tense drift (a state moving under the writer), what the accents are spent
on. Then the trajectory: the state per paragraph with its cause. A flat
line is the machine voice; a jump without a cause is a seam; a trajectory
that moves with the ledger is the mechanism of the reader's feeling, the
thing to protect in revision.

The help mode: when the writer has not said what state the piece should
present, show the one read, then two or three the piece could present
instead, each as one line of the draft rewritten in that state. The pick
is the signal. Never pick for them, never offer more than three, and never
tell them what they feel.

## Why machine prose has no state

A model's default is helpful, even, mildly pleased, about a 2, with no
history and nothing at stake. Uniform density is what a mind with no state
sounds like, at the level of the voice. Setting a state is the strongest
single move out of machine prose, because every other rule follows from
it: the state chooses the details, the details set the length, the length
sets the rhythm, and the rhythm is what the reader feels. The "add soul"
instruction in other guides is this done with a word list; the mindset
does it with the mechanism.

## Whose line is it

The state reaches the page through attention: which details get picked,
how long the sentences run. It never reaches the page as commentary on
the telling. A narrator who says what others leave out and they keep in,
or who flags their own honesty, has an opinion about themselves, and the
reader did not come for it. Ask of any narrator's line who it is for: the
reader (it pays or raises something in the story), a character (it is in
their voice), or the teller (cut it). The same holds for a game's
narrator: opinions on the baron and the pigs, none on the narration.

## The same chords

A page that hums is usually cured with new material: another figure,
another event, a fresh word where the old one stood. That spends the pool
and leaves the hum, because the hum was never the material. It was one
state holding through every section. Keep the chords and turn the hand
that plays them: the same three chords are three sections when the state
behind them changes, and each section sounds like a different thing
because a different mind is looking. The instance the rule came from: a
song written on three chords and one note in the left hand, which a
bandmate first heard as boring, became three distinct sections in the
arrangement without a chord being changed (Eli, 2026-11-04). Hold the
material; move the state; the move is the story.

## The revision

The state moving is the story, and the revision is the state moving in
view: the narrator opens a reading, meets the next fact, and replaces the
reading where the reader can watch. It is staged, since a narrator who
knows the whole story has nothing to learn, and the staging is for the
reader, a guided re-read at the scale of a sentence. The reader keeps the
move. A narrator whose opinion in the last paragraph is the opinion of the
first has told the reader nothing they could not have told themselves.
