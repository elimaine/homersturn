# The wheel: the mindset on the circle of fifths

The eight states in `mindset.md` had no distances between them, so a turn
from anger to fear cost the same on paper as a turn from anger to ease,
and on the page it does not. This is the map. It keeps the arithmetic of
the circle of fifths exactly, so anything true of keys is true of states:
a neighbour is one accidental away, the far side is a tritone, a relative
shares every note, a parallel shares the tonic, a mode is one degree
altered. The emotion wheels gave the shape, Plutchik's eight primaries in
opposite pairs with intensity on the radius and Willcox's six cores with
nuance on the rim. The circle gave the math.

## The twelve positions

Each position is a signature: what attention is on. It holds a bright
state and a dark one, as C major holds A minor, the same details read in
the other mood. The last column is the one accidental to the next
position round, the one thing a neighbour turn changes.

| # | Bright | Dark | The signature: what both attend to | To the next, one feature |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ease | Tiredness | the ordinary, in order; surfaces; what remains to be done | the other person enters |
| 2 | Tenderness | Anger | the other person's hands, mouth, choice; what they did unasked | their word |
| 3 | Trust | Suspicion | what the other said, against what they did | the claim itself |
| 4 | Conviction | Insistence | the claim and its evidence; the objection | the self making it |
| 5 | Pride | Shame | the self under other eyes; what one did | the exits |
| 6 | Curiosity | Fear | thresholds, exits, what is behind, sounds | what is coming |
| 7 | Anticipation | Dread | the next thing; time ahead | it arrives unlooked for |
| 8 | Surprise | Shock | the thing not expected; the sentence that stops | its size |
| 9 | Awe | Terror | size, light, time; nothing human; the self gone | what is here |
| 10 | Joy | Sorrow | what is here, or gone: the cup, the chair, the small kept thing | the body |
| 11 | Abandon | Maudlin | the near and physical; the one person; the same thought three times | the mouth |
| 12 | Relish | Disgust | texture, smell, the mouth; toward, or away | sated: the ordinary |

Opposites sit six apart: ease and anticipation, tenderness and surprise,
trust and awe, conviction and joy, pride and abandon, curiosity and
relish. Two of Plutchik's oppositions are kept as neighbours or relatives
instead, on purpose. Joy and sorrow share every detail and differ in the
mood, so they are a relative pair. Anticipation and surprise are one
feature apart, the thing foreseen and the thing not.

The old eight sit on it: ease 1 bright, tiredness 1 dark, tenderness 2
bright, anger 2 dark, insistence 4 dark, fear 6 dark, awe 9 bright,
abandon and maudlin at 11. What each does to attention, the
sentence, the accents and the tempo is still the table in `mindset.md`.
The wheel adds only where they stand.

## The math, same as notes

- A step round is a fifth. One accidental, one feature of the
  mechanism. One therefore or but pays for it.
- The relative flip is the same number, the other mood: the same
  details, the other hand. It is the cheapest strong move on the wheel,
  since the material is already the reader's. The cold note that cracks
  is anger's relative flip, and the mindset reference calls it a punch.
- The parallel is the same tonic, the other mood, three steps round:
  the bright state at n and the dark state at n − 3, the dark at n and
  the bright at n + 3. Same thing attended, new details chosen. It is the
  revision figure's home, the narrator re-reading one thing. The pairs
  the arithmetic gives are the check on the ordering: fear and awe,
  suspicion and curiosity, ease and sorrow, joy and dread, tenderness and
  maudlin, anger and pride, shock and abandon. Where a pair reads false,
  the ordering is wrong there, not the rule.
- The tritone is six steps, no shared signature. Only through a bridge
  paragraph, all momentum and no new fact, or a payment that size.
- The semitone is seven steps one way, five the other, and Camelot's
  "+7": the jolt that is dissonant on purpose. The twist, the swear.
  Once a piece, late.
- Dynamics are the degree. 1 is piano, 5 is fortissimo. The swear is
  the sforzando. The crescendo figure is the crescendo. A jump of three
  with no cause is a subito, and a subito is melodrama unless the ledger
  paid for it.
- Felt and shown. The felt state is the key and the shown is the dynamic.
  Held is the key felt and played piano, anger's details at ease's
  volume, and the gap is a signal the reader reads.
- Transposition is the same arc from another start, which the skill
  already has as the parallel figure: two characters given the same beat
  in different keys.
- Tonicization is one sentence in another state inside a paragraph
  that does not change key. Mode mixture is a tint, below.

What does not map: a key signature says which seven of twelve are in
play, and a state has no such count. The "one feature" of a neighbour
turn is a judgement, made where the mark goes.

## Modes are tints

A mode is the same signature from another home, one degree altered. So a
tint is a core with one detail or the cadence turned, never a new state,
and the rim of the wheel is seven deep at every position without a word
list. Mark it after the name: `[state: fear phrygian 3]`.

| Mode | Altered degree | The tint, in the mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| Ionian | none | the bright core |
| Aeolian | none | the dark core |
| Lydian | the fourth raised | bright, with one detail too bright: the uncanny in a sunny page |
| Mixolydian | the seventh flat | bright, and the paragraph will not land; easy, loose, the release withheld |
| Dorian | the sixth raised | dark, with one bright detail kept: wistful |
| Phrygian | the second flat | dark, with the thing pressing from just above home: menace, close |
| Locrian | no stable home | nothing lands, fragments, no cadence: panic. Once, and leave |

Scales play the same. A scale is the state's details in order from home,
the attention column of the mindset table, and a passage that walks them
one by one while the degree climbs is the musical sentence. A sequence,
the same figure a step higher each time, is the recursive paragraph and
the crescendo.

## The moves, with their cost

A lazy model takes no turn, or takes the jolt by reflex. This is the
quick reference, as Camelot's wheel is for a DJ: which steps are safe,
which jar, and what each costs.

| Move | Steps | Price | Reads as |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hold | 0 | none; rhythm holds | the form holding; a hum by the fifth paragraph |
| Relative flip | 0, mood | a payment, no new cause | restraint failing, or composure regained |
| Neighbour | 1 | one therefore or but | the story moving |
| Lift | 2 | the "and closer" join | escalation |
| Parallel | 3, mood | the next fact | the revision: the same thing re-read |
| Far | 4 to 6 | a bridge paragraph | a turn earned, or a seam |
| Jolt | 5 or 7, mood | once a piece, late | the twist, the swear |
| Degree up three | any | never without one of the above | melodrama, or a seam |

The script prices a marked draft: `--wheel` prints this map, and the
ledger run reports a crossing with no `[join: bridge]` on it or the
paragraph before, and a degree that jumps three.

## Theory to prose, for the Poet surface

Poet's units already are the music's. The table is the shared vocabulary
for the margin, the move and the copy.

| Poet or homersturn | Music |
|---|---|
| a span's ledger: the plain reading plus the readings by suit | a chord: the root plus the chord tones |
| a reading's strength, silent to loud to told | voicing; told is the root doubled in every voice |
| a paragraph | a phrase |
| a section | a key area |
| raise, plant, pay | dominant, predominant, tonic |
| land | the perfect authentic cadence |
| release | the plagal cadence |
| refrain | the chorus returning; the recapitulation |
| a paragraph ending on a raise | the half cadence |
| the twist, the revision | the deceptive cadence |
| the suspension figure | the suspension, borrowed exactly |
| a state turn at a paragraph break | a direct modulation |
| the revision figure, one sentence in both states | a pivot chord |
| the repeated noun at a seam | a common-tone modulation |
| tempo, rhythm, the forms | tempo, rhythm, the forms |
