They lost by one point and nobody talked on the bus.
Coach sat at the front with the clipboard face down on his knee.
Somewhere past the bridge a phone went off and the kid whose phone it was turned it off without looking at it.
Mara had taken the last shot.
It had looked good leaving her hand, and it had looked good for most of the way, and then it had hit the back of the rim and gone straight up and come down on the wrong side.
She was not thinking about that.
She was thinking about the pass before it, which she had taken a half-second too late, so that the shot had to be rushed, and about the pass before that, which had been fine.
The bus pulled into the school lot and the lights were off in the gym.
Coach stood up and turned around and looked at them.
He said the shot was the right shot.
He said he would have taken it himself.
Then he said that practice was at seven and that he wanted the clipboard back before anyone left.
Mara gave it to him.
