The harbour was empty when Ines came down to it. She had expected boats, because her mother had told her about boats, and there were none. There was a wall, and a flight of green steps going down into the water, and a ring set in the stone with nothing tied to it.

She sat on the top step and waited for something to explain itself. A gull landed on the ring and looked at her with one eye and then the other. After a while a man came along the wall with a bucket, and he did not look at her at all.

He went down the steps past her and tipped the bucket into the water and came back up. The water took whatever it was without a sound. She asked him where the boats were.

He said there had never been boats, and that her mother had been telling her a story. Then he said that the story was a good one and he had heard it too, as a boy, from his own mother. He went away along the wall with the empty bucket. Ines stayed on the step until the tide came up to her shoes.
