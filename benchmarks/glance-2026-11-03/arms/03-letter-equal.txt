I should have written sooner, and I know it. The truth is that nothing happened for most of the summer and I did not want to send you a letter about nothing. Then everything happened in one week and I did not have the time.

The house sold. The buyers are a young couple who stood in the kitchen and said nothing for a long minute, and then she asked whether the pear tree came with it, and I said that it did. That was the whole negotiation.

I have moved the boxes to Dan's garage and I am sleeping in his spare room, which is fine, and which is also not where I expected to be at this age. Mum's things went to the three of us in roughly the way she wanted. I kept the clock.

You will say I always wanted the clock, and you are right, and I have it now, and it is loud at night in a way I had forgotten. I will come up in October if the car lasts. Write back. Tell me about the garden, and whether the fox came back.
