We spent a week choosing a name and most of that week was wasted. The first day we listed everything the product did and tried to find a word that covered all of it, which produced long compound names that nobody could say aloud. The second day we tried the opposite and looked for one short word with no meaning at all, so that the product could fill it.

Those names were easy to say and impossible to remember. On the third day someone asked what the product was for, which is a different question from what it does. It was for keeping a promise.

A person says what they will do, and the thing holds them to it, and nothing else. Once we had that, the long list collapsed. Half the names were about doing and could go.

Half of what remained were about tools, and a promise is not a tool. We were left with four words, and we argued about those for two more days, and it did not matter which one we chose. Any of the four would have worked. The name was found on the third day, when the question changed.
