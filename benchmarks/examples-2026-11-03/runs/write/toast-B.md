## Piece

Thirty years Dana ran this library, and in thirty years she never once closed it for snow.

Think about what that means. Snow closes everything. That is the one thing it is good at. It did not close Dana. Whether anyone came or not, the door was open, and the town knew the door was open, and I think that is what a library is: the one door in town you never have to wonder about.

So here is what I want to say, Dana. You never once closed for snow. You are allowed to now. Sleep in. Let it pile up. Whatever you do with the time, the rest of us have had thirty years to learn from you what open looks like, and we are not going to forget it.

To Dana, who never once closed.
