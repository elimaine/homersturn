## Ledger

Brief: an error screen, read on a phone mid-checkout by someone who can leave at this exact moment. Plain meaning: the bank said no; nothing happened to their money; two ways forward. Under it: nothing. Ambiguity 1 (an error message), mindset: the clerk at the counter, calm, 1, no feeling shown because none is theirs to show, reader strength 1 (tired, annoyed, thumb already over the back button). Point at the top, bad news first. One inference taken: a decline means no charge, so "Nothing was charged" is paid from what a decline is, not from anything the brief supplied; flagged below, cut it if the system cannot promise it. Not taken: why the bank declined (not supplied, so not guessed), "contact your bank" (the brief gives two routes, not three), "sorry" (the line would be for the teller).

Use another card · Try again

Analyze pass on the draft: "declined this card" closes the reading that the payment failed on our side; "your bank" closes the reading that we declined it. "Try it again" names the same card so it does not read as "try paying again by any means", which would duplicate the second button. No dash, no hedge, no adjective, no frame repeated. The stress position of the body is "a different card", the thing most changed from where the reader stands. The one open question is the inferred line; the ledger marks it.

## Piece

**Your bank declined this card.**

Nothing was charged. You can try it again, or use a different card.

Use another card · Try again
