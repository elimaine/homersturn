## Piece

Ferris slept on my chest every night for nineteen years. I would lie down and he would come, and I would lie down and he would come, and there is nothing to say about that except that it was nineteen years of nights and he never once missed. All those nights he was the last thing I felt before sleep and the first thing I felt waking, a weight, warm, over the heart, as if he had chosen the spot. I do not know if he chose it. I know he kept it. So today we take the weight off my chest, which is what people say when something is over, and it is over, and it is not a relief. Tonight I will lie down, and nothing will come.
