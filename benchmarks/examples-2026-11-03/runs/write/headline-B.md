## Ledger

Brief: a local news post; the reader can leave at the headline. Plain meaning: the Fourth Street bridge closes Monday, for six months, for repairs. Under it: nothing held, the headline raises the when and the first line pays the how long and the why. Dials taken from the purpose table: ambiguity 2, mindset a desk reporter at 1 (state never shown, nothing at stake for the teller), strength 3. No detail added that the brief did not supply.

```
    debt: 0   feeling: none   state: plain 1

¶1  owed: how long, and why (¶0)   due now: both
    debt: 5 words   feeling: none   state: plain 1 → plain 1, because ¶0 raised only a fact
    → summary · one sentence, one stop · payment · plain · land
```

[raise: when, and left open: how long, why] Fourth Street Bridge Closes Monday

[join: therefore] [pay: why, in the topic position] Repairs will keep it closed [pay: how long, in the stress position] for six months. [state: plain 1]

Shape: two lines, each taken whole at a glance. The headline carries the fact the reader can leave on; the line ends on the number, the thing that changed for them. Readings checked: "it" has one candidate; "closed" repeats "closes" and is kept, since a rename would cost a re-read. Nothing invented: no date, no detour, no agency.

## Piece

**Fourth Street Bridge Closes Monday**

Repairs will keep it closed for six months.
