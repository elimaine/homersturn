## Ledger

Brief: a game turn; the reader acted and waits, and owes the turn a fact, a cost, a reaction, a threat, a route. Plain meaning: the lock gives, the player goes down, the ledger is there. Under it: "asleep" is a claim the cellar will test. Dials: ambiguity 3, narrator watchful at 3 (second person, present, no opinion on the telling), reader strength 3 (a player at a keyboard, mid-session). Taken from the brief: the ledger is in the cellar, the innkeeper is above; everything else is the turn's invention, which fiction allows.

```
¶1  owed: nothing (the player's act is the raise)   due now: the lock, the intent kept
    debt: 0   feeling: held breath   state: fear 2 → 3, because the third pin fights
    → scene · short stops · plain to lean · no punch · plant where he sleeps

¶2  owed: where he sleeps (¶1), the click (¶1)   due now: the reaction, the cost
    debt: 60 words   feeling: dread, then relief   state: fear 3 → 4 → 2, because the bed creaks, but only once more
    → scene · staccato count · lean · no punch

¶3  owed: the ledger   due now: the fact, the route
    debt: 50 words   feeling: ease, cold   state: fear 2, held, because he is asleep
    → scene · joined sentences · plain · no punch · raise the pen

¶4  owed: "asleep" (¶1, ¶2), the pen (¶3)   due now: the threat
    debt: 140 words since the creak   feeling: the floor tilts   state: fear 2 → 3, but not named
    → pause · two sentences · punch: land
```

You kneel at the cellar door with the wrench in your left hand and the pick in your right. [raise: the pick, a thing that can be spent] Three pins. The first two set like they wanted to. The third fights, and you hold your breath for it, because the innkeeper sleeps in the room above the cellar, not above the bar. [plant: "above the cellar"] [state: fear 3] The third sets. The bolt draws back with a click that is louder than it should be. [raise: the click]

[join: therefore] Above you a bed creaks. [pay: above the cellar, as threat] You count. One. Two. [state: fear 4] At seven the bed creaks again, the long creak of a man turning over. After it, nothing. He is still asleep. [pay: the click, as reaction] [plant: "asleep", second time] Your pick came out of the third pin bent. It will take one more lock, not two. [pay: the cost]

[join: therefore] The door swings in on a stair going down into cold that smells of earth and vinegar. [state: fear 2] Nine steps. Barrels, a trestle table, and on the table under a candle stub the ledger lies open, a pen laid in the gutter of the page. [pay: the fact] [raise: the pen, so he writes in it here] Behind the barrels a coal hatch shows a square of greyer dark where the yard is. [pay: the route]

[join: but] The candle is out. The tallow at its lip is still soft. [pay: the pen, and "asleep" re-read; land]

## Piece

You kneel at the cellar door with the wrench in your left hand and the pick in your right. Three pins. The first two set like they wanted to. The third fights, and you hold your breath for it, because the innkeeper sleeps in the room above the cellar, not above the bar. The third sets. The bolt draws back with a click that is louder than it should be.

Above you a bed creaks. You count. One. Two. At seven the bed creaks again, the long creak of a man turning over. After it, nothing. He is still asleep. Your pick came out of the third pin bent. It will take one more lock, not two.

The door swings in on a stair going down into cold that smells of earth and vinegar. Nine steps. Barrels, a trestle table, and on the table under a candle stub the ledger lies open, a pen laid in the gutter of the page. Behind the barrels a coal hatch shows a square of greyer dark where the yard is.

The candle is out. The tallow at its lip is still soft.
