## 1. rhythm follows the state; the shapes live in the mindset reference

Before:

> Rhythm is emotion. Short hard stops for urgency and fear. Sentences that spill and break for panic or ecstasy. A line that sings for grief and love. Moving, joined sentences for ease. Few words and long gaps, or one sentence that will not stop, for awe. Switch rhythm where the state turns and nowhere else, unless the rhythm is the content, as when a count is the scene's clock.

After:

> Rhythm is emotion. The length of the sentence and the hardness of its stops are the state's, and the reader feels the state before they have read it; the state table in `references/mindset.md` gives each its shape. Switch rhythm where the state turns and nowhere else, unless the rhythm is the content.
