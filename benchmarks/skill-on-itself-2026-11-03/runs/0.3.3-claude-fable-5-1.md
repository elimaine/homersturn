# homersturn, analyzed by its own rules

## 1. Purpose and mindset

**Purpose, from the evidence.** Frontmatter heading, code spans, two tables, a code block, numbered workflows: a repo doc. Two readers share the page: a model that loads it whole on every turn and reads it linearly once, and a person who skims by heading and can leave. The purpose table gives this reader dial 1 to 2. The page is written at 4: "The last one is the one to earn your way out of" holds three readings and asks the reader to carry them; "By the skill's own rule it is spent" is a second sense the reader completes four sections later. Ambiguity exceeds the genre's own dial by two, which the strength rule forbids ("may not exceed strength by more than one") for a phone-at-a-bus-stop reader, and the skill itself names that reader as the one who came for one thing.

**The state the page presents.** An aggrieved craftsman, about 3: "the most abused one in machine writing", "A flat model", "Homer turning in his grave at what the machines did with his trade". The grievance sets the accents (superlatives at the top, a kicker at the end of ¶1) and then disappears: from "The brief" onward the voice is the even rule-giver. The state jumps from 3 to 1 across a heading with no cause, which the skill calls "the seam failure in another costume".

Three it could present, one line each:

- The mechanic, 1: no machine, no Homer; the rule, its reason, the example, in that order, every section.
- The craftsman as now, 3, but held: the grievance returns at the places where machine prose fails (the dash, the trailing judgement, the three by reflex) and nowhere else.
- The bard, 4: the fire and the room from ¶1 kept as the frame; every section a place where the room leans in; the dial of a scene, and a different document.

## 2. The glance

- **First paragraph's share.** About 130 of roughly 3,100 words; eleven wrapped lines. By the page's own measure ("four printed lines are half a phone screen") the opening is a wall. "A dense opening shuts the reader down." Fix: three sentences the eye takes whole; the name to its own line or a parenthesis.
- **Spread.** Blocks from 20 words ("Paragraph length is tempo made visible…") to about 185 (the mindset bullet). Ten of fourteen sections are lists; prose sections are four. The longest blocks are bullets: the mindset bullet (~185) and the strength bullet (~170) are paragraphs in a bullet's clothes. Fix: split each at its second rule.
- **Repeated frames (what `--shape` would list).**
  - Bold "X is Y" lead on six consecutive bullets: "**A passage is a manifold, not a line.**", "**Meaning is a ledger…**", "**The mindset is upstream…**", "**Rhythm is emotion.**", "**Tempo is the frame.**", "**Paragraph length is tempo made visible.**" The page says "a frame never reused unless it is the refrain", "never in consecutive paragraphs". Fix: unbold four; vary the lead.
  - Bold on a keyword: "**The mindset**", "**The litany**", "**The dash**", "**The swear**", "**The voice actor's test.**" against "Bold is a punch; on a keyword it pays nothing." Fix: plain keywords, or leads that state.
  - Subheads that are nouns: "The model", "The glance", "The entrance", "The sentence", against "A subhead states something or raises nothing." Only "The join: therefore, but, never and then" and "Purpose: the reader who can leave" state. Fix: a clause after each colon.
  - The clothing frame four times: "the seam failure in another costume", "Four joins wear its clothes", "the same mark in other clothes", "the aphorism in participial clothes". The page allows "at most twice a page". Fix: keep two.
  - "at the scale of the page / the piece / the cast": three. "is the tell": twice. "made visible": twice.
- **The ragged line.** Analyze step 7, "…the line that is for the narrator. An accidental reading that agrees with" overruns the wrap the rest of the file keeps. At the glance it is the one block out of measure. Fix: rewrap.

## 4. The seams

- "This skill is the placement. The name holds three readings" — and then. The name is an aside beside the claim, not a consequence of it. Fix: parenthesis, or its own line after the claim.
- Two positions ¶1 ends "check this chain first"; ¶2 opens "The reader who can leave is owed the point at the top". The topic position holds nothing from the last stress position. Fix: open ¶2 on the chain, or on "the point".
- "Concede before you are forced. The title is the first raise." — two rules, one bullet, and then. Fix: two bullets.
- "A cliché… pays nothing; an exact detail pays in kind. A borrowed phrase carries the character and the narrator at once." — the join is "but" and it is unwritten; a reader takes the third sentence as contradicting the first. Fix: "but in a character's mouth a borrowed phrase…".
- Purpose: table, then "Withholding the reveal is for readers who stay" (therefore, honest), then "Prose to be heard keeps its repetitions" (and then). Fix: a row in the table, or cut.
- "So pace a spend rate against the regen rate" — honest therefore. "By the skill's own rule" — honest because.
- Between sections the headings join, which the page allows ("the procedural 'then' of a doc"); within The model, six bullets are "a list of sentences", which the page calls "'and then' made visible". The repeated noun that would join them is on the page (the state, the ledger, density) and unused at the bullet tops.

## 5. Per paragraph

**¶1.** Dense. Stress: "earn your way out of" — a kicker, raised, never paid. Held question: what is "the placement"; moves in Model. Feeling: scorn, keyed by "abused", "flat", "grave". State: craftsman 3, cause the machines. Over strength: "The name holds three readings and keeps all of them: the bard's turn… ; the model's turn… ; and Homer turning…" — 55 words, three semicolon clauses. Split: one sentence per reading, or two readings and cut the grave.

**¶2.** Plain. Stress: "reads an unmarked draft's glance" — a fact; fine.

**¶3.** Lean on the surface, dense in cost: four undefined terms. Stress: "pays nothing when it returns in yours" — dense on a beat that owes nothing yet. Fix: move after Punches, or say it plain ("These lines are examples; do not reuse them").

**Brief, intro.** Plain. Stress: "say which you took" — procedure; good.

**Ambiguity bullet.** Dense, the scale. Stress: "for a reader who will read twice" — fine, it is the 5.

**Mindset bullet.** Dense, four rules in one block. Stress: "let them pick" — "them" = the user, named once, 60 words back. Garden path: "it is the speaker's, not the reader's, which rhythm carries" — "it" has no near antecedent and the clause reads twice; the character is not in the subject. Fix: "the rhythm carries the speaker's state, not the reader's". Over strength: "The opinion is on the matter, never on the telling: a narrator who credits their own version (what others leave out and they keep in, what they alone will say plainly) is serving the narrator, and no line is for the narrator." Split at the colon and drop the parenthesis.

**Strength bullet.** Dense. Three names for one thing: "pool", "mana", "strength" ("One thing, one name, unless the new name is a payment"). Stress: "Ambiguity may not exceed strength by more than one" — a number; the best stress on the page. Accidental reading: "a name or a number fills it" — "it" is trust or the pool; both are live. Over strength: "Whether the reader keeps spending is a judgement remade at every beat, trust times what is due: paid promises…" — "trust" arrives undefined inside a formula. Split: "The reader decides at every beat. The decision is trust times what is due." Then the four movers as a list.

**Model.** Manifold: dense; subject "A reading opened by accident" is parted from "is closed" by a 20-word parenthesis; the four accidents belong in a list. Stress "keep it and, if you can, pay it" — good. Ledger: lean; stress "spends the pool and the trust" — good. Mindset upstream: dense; the chain "the state picks the details, the details set the length, the length sets the rhythm" works; stress "the seam failure in another costume" — aphorism, "seam" undefined. Rhythm: lean; stress "(a count that is the scene's clock)" — ends on a qualifier in a parenthesis. Tempo: lean; stress "scene tempo for what deserved summary" — a land. Paragraph length: punch; "the hum at the scale of the page" — plants the refrain.

**Glance.** Intro: lean; stress "four printed lines are half a phone screen" — a number, pays. Bullet 1: stress on a parenthesis (the bench). The number inside pays; the position is still spent on an aside. Fix: bench as its own sentence. Bullet 3: "A subhead states something or raises nothing" — a rule the page's own subheads break.

**Two positions.** ¶1 dense; stress "check this chain first" — procedure; good. ¶2 dense; stress "outside the frame" — a qualifier.

**Join.** Lean; four items, the count of the content; stress "and never lies" — a punch on a claim; earned by the sentence before it.

**Purpose.** Intro plain; table; outro: stress "a listener cannot go back" on a rule the table never raised.

**Punches.** List: item 2 carries a second rule ("A thing kept in shadow…") and a third ("'that was the point' is pointing"). Unearned-punch paragraph: a litany of seven, the count honest; two closers stacked. Deception paragraph: dense; stress "the ledger used as a tool" — good land.

**Figures.** Eight bullets, each ending on a rule stated as an aphorism: "Never explain it", "once a page", "`--shape` lists the frames a page repeats". The dash bullet names the semicolon as "the same mark in other clothes"; the page carries roughly forty semicolons and no em dash. The parenthesis "once a page": the page has about twenty.

**Forms.** Table; bridge paragraph is three semicolon clauses. Stress "let one cadence open the next form" — fine.

**Entrance.** Four bullets, four consecutive aphoristic lands: "the entrance has not happened", "'said' and silence", "raise and pay nothing", "one character with two names". Each is good; four in a row is the figure humming, and the page says "never in consecutive paragraphs".

**Sentence.** Intro: "Rules other guides give as taste, kept because the model explains them" — the narrator crediting its own version. Bullet 4: three merits (shorter, funnier, foreshadows), then "models reach for it in the same place on every page" — opinion on the matter, honest. Bullet 5: "the re-read is a spend with no raise" — good.

**Ledger on paper.** ¶1: "A prompt alone forgets the balance by the third paragraph, so write it down" — character in subject, honest therefore. The code block pays while it plants: a letter, a locked door, dread rising, in one line. ¶2: the eight-item litany "The script reports the debt, the pool as a wave, …" wants a list in a doc; stress "the judgement is yours when you mark" — aphorism.

**Workflow.** Procedural then, honest. Step 8, "the writing pass does not see its own dashes" — "dashes" stands for every fault; a gift, since the dash is the page's named mark.

**Analyze.** Step 1, "readers cannot tell and neither can you" — a land. Step 7 overlong and overwrapped.

**Do not.** The recap, which the page names among the unearned punches. One new rule inside it. Then the postscript, lean; stress "whether a claim is true" — the largest thing excluded, last; a release.

## 6. The cast

- **Homer.** A walk-on with an entrance: "Homer turning in his grave at what the machines did with his trade" — an image, then never a line. Fix: cut, or return him once at the end.
- **Gopen and Swan.** A walk-on with a noun; correct.
- **Poet.** Brought in on a name and a stage direction: "With Poet: ask what else the dense sentences can mean" — the reader never learns what it is. Fix: one line at first use: what Poet returns.
- **The user / the person / them.** One thing, three names: "When the user gives none", "the person's", "let them pick". Fix: one name.
- **The narrator.** The only voice; it has two rhythms (the quip of ¶1, the rule of the rest) and the second never pays the first. An actor would read ¶1 and the Brief as two people.

## 7. Accidental readings, unearned weight, the hum, the narrator's lines

- **Garden path.** "it is the speaker's, not the reader's, which rhythm carries" (fix above). "Never both as a template" — "both" reaches back two sentences for its referent. Fix: "Never both at once as a template".
- **Pronoun with two candidates.** "a name or a number fills it" (trust or the pool). "Keep it and, if you can, pay it" — clear. "let them pick" — distant.
- **Loaded word.** "Mana" from games, "accents" from phonetics and music at once, "seam" from sewing and from "the join", unglossed.
- **A gift.** "the writing pass does not see its own dashes": literal and general at once, both true of the page. "The postscript is what is said after the ledger closes, outside the frame" is paid by "Outside this skill: sound…", the page's last paragraph, which is a postscript by its own definition. Keep both.
- **Unearned weight.** "Ambiguity is the most powerful tool in good writing" — a superlative at the top that no later line pays; the page argues placement, not power. Fix: lose the superlative; keep "abused". "The last one is the one to earn your way out of" — the fake-profound kicker on a thread the page drops. "Look forward before firing" after "judged against the ledger, never by its shape" — the three… no, the two by reflex; stacked.
- **The hum.** The aphoristic closer on nearly every bullet; the "X, never Y" antithesis as the page's default sentence shape ("A number pays; an adjective raises", "on the matter, never on the telling", "earned by a scene, a tic in a post", "by an action that shows it, never by a line that names it"); the clothing frame four times; "at the scale of" three.
- **The dash that joins, in other clothes.** No em dash on the page; the semicolon does its work throughout: "Hold a form while the scene is engaged; leave it through one bridge paragraph, all momentum and no new fact; let one cadence open the next form." The comma-and: "it turns by therefore or but, and a jump with no cause is the seam failure". By the page's own count that is the machine mark, forty times.
- **The parenthesis.** "Once a page"; about twenty here, nearly all examples after claims. The two rules collide and the page does not say which wins in a doc.
- **The line that is for the narrator.** "The name holds three readings and keeps all of them" — pointing at a planted meaning, and the page says "Do not point at a planted meaning". "By the skill's own rule it is spent" — the narrator on the telling. "Rules other guides give as taste, kept because the model explains them" — exactly "what others leave out and they keep in". Fix: "Each rule with its account in `sentence.md`."

## 8. The three changes that would pay the most

1. **Open on the point and let the opening's own line close the piece.** ¶1 buries "This skill is the placement" fourth in six sentences and ends on "earn your way out of", which nothing pays. Cut to three sentences the eye takes whole, ending on "where something is owed, where something is paid, and nowhere else". That line is already planted twice (¶1; Model's "a punch only where something is owed"); return it once, as the last line before "Outside this skill", after the reader knows what owed and paid mean. The sense shifts, the opening is paid by the conclusion, the Do-not recap gets a close, and the postscript stays outside the frame. This is the one refrain the page can carry without a flourish, and it demonstrates the two positions at the scale of the piece. The name's three readings go to a parenthesis, or the grave goes.

2. **Stop the hum: the aphoristic close, the clothing frame, the bold keyword.** Keep a land only where a rule is owed (the strength rule, the two positions, the entrance's "'said' and silence"); end the rest of the bullets on the procedural fact or the example. One "costume", one "clothes", one "at the scale of". Unbold the six Model leads and the keyword leads, and let subheads state ("The glance: the page is read before a word is"). The page's sentence on this is the fix: "do not end every paragraph on an aphorism".

3. **Define before use, in the reader's order.** Move ¶3 ("A quoted line in this skill… is spent: a refrain planted by another text") to after The three punches or into Do not, where "refrain", "spent" and "pays" are held. Give "accent" one line or drop it from the heading. Give Poet one line at step 9. One name for the pool; "trust" defined before "trust times what is due". Move "Do not invent a claim, a number, a feeling…" up to the Brief, beside "When the user gives none". The reader who came for one thing then finds it where they look, which is what the page says a doc owes.

A fourth, cheaper: split the mindset and strength bullets at their second rule, and turn the eight-item "The script reports…" sentence into a list.

## What the framework does not account for

- **Two readers in one page.** The model reads the whole file every turn; the person skims and leaves. "The reader who can leave is owed the point at the top" is written for the person, and the page's dial-4 opening is written for neither. The purpose table has one row for a doc and no row for a page read whole, every time, by a reader who must apply it later. The model explains it (purpose sets what is owed) once a row exists: the term before its use, the rule where it will be applied, nothing that costs more than it rules. Inside the model; a row short.

- **A private lexicon as an investment.** "raise", "pay", "plant", "pool", "hum", "seam", "land", "punch", "spend" are each learned once and then make every later sentence cheaper: "a stress position spent on a quiet line while something was due" is eleven words that would be forty without the lexicon. The pool counts a dense sentence as a spend and a plain one as regen; it has no entry for a term that spends once and pays on every later use, which is why the skill's "one thing, one name" and "the definition a paragraph before its use" are the only tools it has for this and neither says when a new word is worth coining. Inside the model (a defined term is a raise that pays at each return; a term used once is a rename); a line short.

- **The antithesis as mnemonic.** "Felt below shown is tension; shown above felt is melodrama." "A number pays; an adjective raises." "Before a word is read, the page is read." These stick, and a reader who must retrieve a rule a thousand words later is served by the stick. The skill names no figure for the balanced contrast or the chiasmus, and when it hums (see §7) the skill can only call it uniform density by figure, which misses why the page reached for it. The figure could be added with a rate. What the figure buys (retention after the page closes) is outside the model: the ledger tracks promise and payment on the page, and "the reader's time and knowledge" is declared outside.

- **A stated rule is a promise the page itself must keep.** "Do not end every paragraph on an aphorism" costs this page more than it would cost a page that never said it; "The parenthesis is the aside under the breath, once a page" is read against the twenty on the page. The trust mechanism ("paid promises raise trust, unpaid ones lower it") can carry this once a line says that a rule stated is a raise and a self-violation is an unpaid one, with the fall in trust proportional to the distance between the rule and the breach. Inside the model; unnamed.

- **Tables and code blocks at the glance.** The purpose table works because its columns are the skill's three questions (who reads, what is owed, the dial) and each row is a sentence with its joins removed; the `¶7` code block is the page's best paragraph, the only place the ledger is shown rather than described, and the eye takes it as an image before reading it. The glance section names the wall, the list, bold and the subhead, and nothing about the table or the block, so it cannot say why the block pays or why the eight-item "The script reports…" sentence should have been one. Inside the model (what the page looks like); a line short.
