# homersturn 0.3.3 analyzed with itself

Shape of each finding: pattern; quoted line; fix in a few words. No score, no guess at the author.

## 1. Purpose and mindset

**Purpose, from the evidence.** Headings, code spans, a reference list, a table, a numbered workflow: a repo doc. Two readers at once: a model at session start, which reads all of it, and a person who maintains it and can leave at any scroll. The piece's own table sets this genre at dial 1 to 2 ("the answer where they will look; one name for one thing; the smallest valid structure").

**Dial the page actually runs at.** 3 to 4. The opening holds three readings of the title and asks the reader to earn one; "hum", "the tell", "turn" are planted and paid across sections.
- Pattern: ambiguity above the genre's dial. Line: "The name holds three readings and keeps all of them". Fix: run the doc at 2; keep one planted line a page.

**Mindset the page presents.** A teacher at state 3: certain, aphoristic, with a held contempt for machine prose that shows once ("Homer turning in his grave at what the machines did with his trade before this"). Opinions mostly on the matter. One place the opinion is on the telling: the skill crediting its own version against "the machines ... before this" and "This skill is the placement." That is the narrator serving the narrator by the piece's own definition ("a narrator who credits their own version ... is serving the narrator").

**Mindsets it could present** (no mindset was asked for):
- The craftsman at 2: each rule with its reason in one clause, no Homer, no "you".
- The exasperated editor at 4: shorter, more imperatives, the contempt shown in the cuts and never said.
- The composer at 3 (the current one): the music figures kept as the one refrain, the grave cut.

## What the framework does not account for

- **A text whose content is its own rule.** "The unearned punch has names: the importance claim, the fake-profound kicker, the recap, the colon before a reveal, ..." The reader who has just read "the colon before a reveal" hears this colon and cannot tell whether the writer noticed. Every line in a self-describing doc is read twice: as prose and as a test of the rule it states; an apparent violation is either a failure or a demonstration, and which one depends on whether the reader credits the writer. The skill has deception (a reading opened knowing it is wrong, paid by a reveal) and the gift (an accident that agrees), but no account of the reading "did they mean it?", which is a reading about the writer, not about the matter. The model could take it as a section: a stated rule is a raise against the page that states it, paid where the page obeys it in the place the reader will look, and an unpaid one costs trust at the rate of a broken promise. Inside the model (the ledger, trust).
- **The genre the piece is, missing from its own table.** "A repo doc, a spec | came for one thing" is the nearest row; this piece is a prompt: read whole by a model with no pool to spend and skimmed by a person who maintains it. Two readers, two pools, two regen rates on one page. The pool dial is one number. The model can hold it (name the purpose from the evidence; two dials, the stricter wins; the rule where the model will look and the reason beside it for the person) and the table needs the row. Inside the model, a section missing.
- **Compression that spends with nothing raised.** "Felt below shown is tension; shown above felt is melodrama." Nothing is owed here; no second sense is held; the cost is syntax removed, not meaning stacked. The pool counts "a dense sentence, a raise, a stacked clause, a reading left open"; elision is none of those and spends like all of them. Inside the model (the pool), one line to add: elision spends like a raise and pays nothing.
- **Mood as a hum.** "A subhead states something or raises nothing." Rule, or observation? Nearly every sentence on the page is a generic present declarative ("Bold is a punch", "Rhythm is emotion"), with the imperative withheld until "Do not". The reader cannot tell what they must do from what the piece has noticed, and the page sounds the same at every line for a reason the skill's hum (length, density, figure) does not name: one verb mood, one person, one tense, across 3,300 words. The mindset section says the state sets the rhythm; mood and person are where a state shows first. Inside the model, as a dial under mindset.
- **The joke as a payment.** "the writing pass does not see its own dashes." It pays: the reader completes the laugh from the dash rule and the dashless page. The skill names the joke only as an entrance move ("a small reveal, a joke") and "funnier by bathos" in passing; the ledger has no entry for the line that pays by making the reader complete something true and absurd at once. Inside the model (a pay by a reading the reader completes), a sentence to add under the punches.
- **Outside the model, by the skill's own line.** "Gopen and Swan", "rondo", "villanelle", "sforzando", "bathos": the piece leans on the reader's knowledge in a dozen places and declares that outside its scope ("the reader's time and knowledge"). Named as outside; nothing to add.
