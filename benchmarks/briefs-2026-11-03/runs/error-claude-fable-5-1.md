## Ledger

Brief: an error message at checkout. Reader is on a phone, mid-purchase, can leave, and has just been told no. Plain meaning: the bank said no to this card, nothing was paid, two ways out. Under it: the no is the card's, not yours and not ours. Dials: ambiguity 1, mindset the product speaking, calm 1, strength 1. None given, all taken from the purpose.

```
¶1 title   owed: nothing   due now: the bad news, first, with its cause
           debt: 0   feeling: a small jolt   state: calm 1
           → one line · plain · stress on "this card" so the second button is already implied · no punch

¶2 body    owed: what happened to the money, what to do   due now: both
           debt: 0   feeling: jolt → ease   state: calm 1, unchanged
           → two short sentences · end on the way out · no punch

¶3 buttons owed: the two routes, in the order the body gave them   due now: both
           → two labels · one name for one thing ("card", never "payment method")
```

Marked draft:

```
Your bank declined this card [state: calm 1] [plant: "this card", closed on the card so "declined" does not read as declined *you*]

No payment went through. [pay: what "declined" means, said in the reader's words] [join: therefore] You can try again, or use a different card. [pay: "this card" → a different card; stress position on the route out]

[Use a different card]  [Try again]
```

Readings closed: "declined" alone can land as a judgement on the person; "this card" puts it on the card. "No payment went through" restates the decline rather than adding a claim about charges. Nothing raised, so nothing owed, so no punch.
