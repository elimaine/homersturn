## Piece

Ferris slept on my chest every night for nineteen years, a small warm weight that rose and fell until I could not tell his breath from mine. He was there when I was sick and when I was well, and he never once asked which. Tonight the weight is gone and the chest keeps rising anyway. We have given him back to the ground, where it is warm. Sleep.
