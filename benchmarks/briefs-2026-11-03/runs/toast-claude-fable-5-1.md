## Piece

Snow shuts a town. The roads, the school, your own front door. For thirty years, it never shut the library. The library was open.

I don't know how Dana got there on those mornings. I'm not sure she'd tell us. But somebody must have walked in from the cold, stamped their boots, and found the lights on and the heat up and Dana at the desk, and that was the whole town, right there, in one warm room that hadn't given in. The library was open.

Thirty years. She never once closed it. Tomorrow somebody else has to learn how not to. Dana — stay home next time it snows. Raise a glass. To Dana. The library was open.
