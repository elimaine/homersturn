## Piece

Thirty years, Dana ran this library, and not once did she close it for snow.

Think about that. The morning the plows gave up, the morning the schools gave up, the morning the whole town agreed to stay in bed — the light over the front desk was already on.

She'll tell you it was only the hours. But I know who came through that door on those days: the kid with nowhere warmer, the old man who needed one human face, the rest of us who came for a book and found we'd come for the quiet.

So here's to Dana. Tomorrow it can snow as hard as it likes. For the first time in thirty years, you get to stay home — and the rest of us will keep the light on.
