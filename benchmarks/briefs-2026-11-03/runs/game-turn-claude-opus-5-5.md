## Piece

The first two pins lift easy. The third fights you, and the pick sweats in your fingers until—there. The bolt draws back with a sound like a swallowed word.

The door swings inward on cold air that smells of earth and old wine. Six stone steps down, the shelves hold the casks and the strongbox and, on the lowest, a thin black book. The innkeeper's ledger.

But above you, through the boards, the innkeeper's snoring catches and stops. A floorboard settles under nothing. Then the snoring finds its rhythm again. The book is twenty steps and one quiet minute away.
