## Piece

The sword was a sewing needle, dropped behind the granary by some careless giant, and the mouse who worked it free of the dust was named Pell, no bigger than your thumb and twice as frightened.

Below the mill there was a kingdom. Not a grand one — only a warren of grain-rooms and nurseries where the field-mice had wintered for a hundred quiet years. They kept a council and a harvest-song and no soldiers at all, because nothing had ever come for them that a shut door would not turn away.

Then came Vashka. He was a thief, I thought at first, a fat soft thief in a coat worth more than everything he stole — and then I learned what he did in the nurseries, and thief was far too clean a word, because a thief takes what you can spare and leaves you your children, and Vashka took the seed-corn and the smallest of them and even their names, which he learned for no reason but to call them out, one by one, into the dark.

The council voted to give him what he asked. Pell did not vote. Pell went down to the granary where the needle lay, because a thing small enough to be overlooked is small enough to get close, and she had decided, at last, that this was a use for being small.

She was waiting at the door when he came out, chinchilla-huge, soft as ash, smiling the way a creature smiles when it has never once been told no. "And what do they call you?" he asked, almost kindly. She gave him the point of the needle instead.

I will not pretend it was a battle. He outweighed her forty times over and she held a sewing needle. But a needle finds the one seam in a thing that believes itself armored, and fear, it turns out, is only courage that has not yet been handed its reason — and Pell had hers now, and she drove the needle home.

When the mill-mice came up at dawn they found Vashka still and cold, and Pell beside him too spent to lift her blade, and they did not yet understand what she had done. Only that the doors stood open. Only that no one, that morning, had been called into the dark.

They build the harvest-song differently now. It is shorter than it was. It is only this: that the smallest of them went down with the smallest of blades, and that it was enough.
