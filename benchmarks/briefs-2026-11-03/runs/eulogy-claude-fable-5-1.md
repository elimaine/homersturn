Analyze pass on the draft: no dash. Comma-and appears three times across seven sentences; one was cut (the fourth sentence was split) so the figure does not hum. "Tonight" opens the last sentence as a bare time noun, but it cannot be taken for the subject because "I will" follows at once, and it pays "every night" twice planted. Closed reading: "chest" as a box for burying was left out of the page by never naming a box or the ground. Kept reading: "the rising and the falling" against the name Ferris, which agrees with the ledger and is not pointed at.

## Piece

Ferris slept on my chest every night for nineteen years. That is something near seven thousand nights, and on each of them he climbed up, turned once, and lay down where my breathing lifted him and let him down again. I learned to fall asleep under that weight. He never wanted the pillow or the foot of the bed, only the chest, only the rising and the falling of it. I have decided that was his way of making sure of me. For nineteen years he had what he wanted every night, and so did I. Tonight I will lie down with nothing on my chest, and for the first time in nineteen years I do not know how to fall asleep.
