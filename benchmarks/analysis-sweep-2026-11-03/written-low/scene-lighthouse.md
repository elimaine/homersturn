## Piece

The storm came up the coast the way it always did, from the south, loud before it was wet. She trimmed the wick and set the flame and watched the beam go out over the water, turning, turning.

She had the kettle on. She had set two cups down from the shelf, the way she set them down every night the weather turned, because he walked the cliff path in anything and came in soaked and grateful and she liked to have the water already hot.

The second cup had a film of dust in it. She wiped it with her thumb and set it back, rim out, the way it had been set out a long time now.

The beam went round. It found the path empty and the water empty and came back to her and found her still standing, which was the only thing the light ever found anymore. She poured one cup. He was late the way the dead are late.
