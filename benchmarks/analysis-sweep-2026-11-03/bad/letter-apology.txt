Dear Sam,

I have been meaning to write to you for a long time, and I owe you a sincere apology. Somewhere along the way, our conversations faded into silence, and I let years slip by without reaching out. That was my failing, not yours, and I am sorry for it.

I think about our old days often: the long drives with the windows down, the late-night talks that never seemed to end, and the way you always knew how to make me laugh when I needed it most. You were a constant in my life, and I allowed that constancy to slip away through my own busyness and neglect. I told myself there would always be time later, but later kept getting pushed back, and I regret that more than I can easily say.

I do not expect you to have waited for me. Friendships take effort from both sides, and I understand if you have moved on or felt hurt by my silence. Whatever your feelings, I would be grateful for the chance to make things right, even if it is simply to hear how your life has been and to share some of mine.

If you are willing, I would love to catch up over coffee, a phone call, or whatever works best for you. There is so much I want to hear about your family, your work, and the adventures you have had since we last spoke.

Thank you for your patience and for the friendship we once shared. I hope we can rebuild it, and I promise to be a better friend this time.

With warm regards and sincere apologies,

Alex
