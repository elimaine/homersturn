# Analysis

The ledger script isn't in this workspace, so the shape counts below were done by hand. The piece is one paragraph of 222 words in four sentences, followed by an 11-word all-caps heading with an asterisk.

## 1. Purpose and mindset

**Genre: an essay reaching its conclusion, at the scale of a long argument. It works as the abstract completed, in recapitulation form.**

The evidence that this is an ending:
- "These laws", "Thus" and "this view of life" all point back at ground the reader has already covered.
- The second sentence restates a whole system as if it had been established.
- A back-matter heading follows.

The reader chose to stay. Several rules turn on that:
- The point belongs at the bottom, and it is there: "evolved" is the last word.
- A dense final block is allowed, because nothing is being opened.
- The litany is borderline, since the skill earns it in scenes and this is an essay.

**Dial: 4.** Three readings are held to the end: "view", "higher", and the Creator clause.

**Tempo:**
- S1 is a pause: time stops on the bank.
- S2 and S3 are summary: all of time in two sentences.
- S4 is a pause at planetary scale that opens back into summary ("are being").

**Mindset: measured awe, held in check by argument, at about 2 of 5.**
- The hedges show the restraint: "taken in the largest sense", "almost implied", "or into one".
- Death is named plainly: "famine and death".
- The human is walked around: "the higher animals".
- The feeling is named only once, late: "grandeur".

You didn't say which mindset you want the piece to present, so this is the help mode. Here is S3/S4 in three states, to pick by ear:

- **Cool, at 1:** "The higher animals are produced by the war of nature, by famine and death."
- **Awe, far gone, at 4:** "Famine, death, and out of them the higher animals. From so simple a beginning, endless forms. Still coming."
- **Combative, at 3:** "Famine and death made the higher animals, and that is grand."

## 2. The glance

- **One block holds 95% of the page.** It is 222 words, about 55 words per sentence, and over twenty lines on a phone. A dense opening would shut a leaving reader out, but this reader arrives by reading, so the wall costs less here than the rule implies.
- **Capitalized terms act like bold.** "Growth… Inheritance… Variability… Ratio of Increase… Struggle for Life… Natural Selection…" Spent on keywords, the skill says this pays nothing. These read as terms of art the body must have defined, so the capitals are the "one thing, one name" made visible. That is a false positive here, provided the body did raise them.
- **The heading spends the page's last glance on a label.** "GLOSSARY OF THE PRINCIPAL SCIENTIFIC TERMS USED IN THE PRESENT VOLUME.*" The eye's last stop is a shout in caps, not "evolved". Fix: whitespace or a page break before the back matter.

## 4. The seams

- **S1→S2: repeated noun.** "…by laws acting around us. / These laws…" This is the invisible join working as the skill describes. No fix needed.
- **S2→S3: therefore, and honest.** "Thus" follows "Extinction of less improved forms", and the next words, "famine and death", restate the extinction. No fix needed.
- **S3→S4: an unannounced "but".** "…directly follows. / There is grandeur in this view." The turn is earned and never announced, as the essay row asks. No fix needed.
- **¶1→heading: neither join.** It is an exit from the frame, closer to a postscript than to a seam. Fix: separate it visually so it doesn't read as the next beat.

## 5. The five measurements

**¶1, measured whole (sentence notes inline):**
- **Band:** dense throughout, landing at the end. S1 starts lean on the image and thickens into abstraction. S2 and S3 are dense. S4 is dense, with a punch in its last clause.
- **Stress position:** "evolved". This is the right word: the process the paragraph has been describing, named last.
  - **S3's stress is spent on the logical verb.** "…the production of the higher animals, directly follows." Fix: end on the dark term, so S4's turn hits harder: "the higher animals… come directly from the war of nature, from famine and death." This costs the sense of inevitability that "directly follows" carries.
- **Held question moved:** yes. "Laws acting around us" is paid twice: S2 says what the laws are, and "are being" says they are still acting.
- **Feeling:**
  - It moves from contemplative (S1) to grim (S3) to awe (S4).
  - The key-up rises steadily.
  - It never spikes, because the restraint holds until "grandeur".
- **Planted line:** two plants are carried.
  - "Forms", four times, with its sense shifting each time: "constructed forms", then "less improved forms" (the extinct), then "a few forms" (the origin), then "endless forms" (the result). It is a one-word refrain, planted three times before it lands.
  - "Law(s)" appears three times. The gravity clause sets the new laws beside an accepted one, which borrows authority.

**Heading:**
- **Band:** plain label.
- **Stress position:** an orphan asterisk.
- **Held question:** none moves.
- **Feeling:** a drop into the reference register.
- **Planted line:** "TERMS" arguably calls back to S2's capitals.

## 6. The topic position

- **S1 opens on a dummy subject.** "It is interesting to contemplate a tangled bank…" The topic arrives at word 7. Fix: "Contemplate a tangled bank…" (this has a cost, discussed below).
- **S2 has no main verb, so the reader reads it twice.** "These laws, taken in the largest sense, being Growth…" The known-new chain is fine; the syntax is what stalls. Fix: "being" → "are".
- **S4 makes the reader hold "that" for about 25 words before its subject.** "…and that, whilst this planet has gone circling on according to the fixed law of gravity, from so simple a beginning endless forms…" The delay is deliberate and partly earned (see the final heading), but "that" has no governing word. Fix: "and in this, that whilst…"

## 7. Accidental readings

- **Dangling participle and a two-candidate pronoun.** "this view of life, with its several powers, having been originally breathed…" What was breathed: life, its powers, or the view? Fix: "life, with its several powers, was originally breathed…"
- **Ambiguous restriction.** "Inheritance which is almost implied by reproduction" can read as restrictive (a kind of inheritance) or as a gloss. Fix: put a comma before "which".
- **A momentary misreading.** "have been, and are being evolved" makes "have been" read briefly as "have existed". Fix: none; the next words close it in time. Keep it.
- **A second sense worth keeping, probably chosen.** "There is grandeur in this view." The bank was literally a view to contemplate; now "view" means the theory. Fix: none.
- **Readings a present-day reader opens.** "less improved forms" and "the higher animals" imply a ranking. Whether the writer opened these is undecidable from the text (see the final heading).
- **The rare word, where the common one would do.** "whilst", "elaborately constructed". These are minor and read as period voice.

## 8. Unearned weight

- **Importance claim and "namely" reveal: earned.** "the most exalted object which we are capable of conceiving, namely, the production of the higher animals" is the reveal-pattern the list names. It pays a displacement: the reader fills "most exalted" with something else, and the line replaces it. It does not pay an ordinary line.
- **Importance claim: earned.** "There is grandeur in this view of life" is the land. The 200 words before it owed it.
- **Recap: earned only conditionally.** S2 is the abstract completed if the body raised these terms. Otherwise it is a list of words that only promise weight.
- **No three by reflex.** Plants, birds, insects and worms make four, swept as bushes, air and earth. "From the war of nature, from famine and death" and "most beautiful and most wonderful" are pairs.
- **The rest are absent:** no denied reading, no joining dash, no swear, no pointing participle. "Entailing Divergence of Character" trails, but it carries cause; it doesn't point.

## 9. The three changes that would pay the most

1. **Give S2 a verb.** "These laws… being Growth" → "These laws… are Growth". The paragraph's one forced reread disappears at the cost of one word.
2. **Fix S4's attachments.** "with its several powers, having been originally breathed…; and that, whilst…" → "life, with its several powers, was originally breathed…; and in this, that whilst…" The final sentence carries the landing and shouldn't make the reader hunt for its grammar.
3. **Take the last glance back from the heading.** "…are being evolved. / GLOSSARY…*" → a page break, and lose the orphan asterisk. Then "evolved" is the last thing the eye holds.

## What the framework does not account for

- **A crescendo across one paragraph.** The evaluative words climb: "interesting" → "elaborately constructed" → "so complex" → "most exalted" → "grandeur" → "most beautiful and most wonderful". The reader's feeling is raised by degrees, so "grandeur" lands as arrived-at rather than asserted.
  - This is also why the skill's fix for S1 ("Contemplate a tangled bank") would cost something. "It is interesting" is the deliberately low first step.
  - The skill has accents (sforzando, rest) and punches, but no gradual dynamic.
  - The model could explain it if a section were added: it is rhythm and placement at the scale of word choice.
- **Long sentences carrying awe.** The rhythm table maps awe to "few words and long gaps". Here the length enacts the content instead. "whilst this planet has gone circling on according to the fixed law of gravity" interrupts the sentence and circles before it arrives, as an orbit would. The reader feels the scale by having to hold it.
  - The model could explain it if rhythm-as-emotion gained a clause for syntax that imitates its subject.
- **The comma as a rest.** In "have been, and are being evolved", the comma splits past from present and gives the last two words their weight. The skill names only the dash as a rest.
  - This is within the model, as timing. A section on punctuation as rests would cover it.
- **Allusion: a raise the reader brings from another text.** "breathed by the Creator into a few forms or into one" and the postposed "forms most beautiful" draw on a creation story and a liturgical cadence the reader already holds. Their weight comes from outside the piece.
  - The ledger only counts promises raised on the page.
  - The model could explain it if it added a column for readings raised by the reader's prior texts and paid by memory.
- **Readings opened by time, not by the writer.** "Less improved", "higher animals" and "whilst" now open readings, such as ranking or period distance, that the accidental-readings check can't separate from readings the writer opened. The common-word rule also assumes the reader shares the writer's present.
  - This is outside the model as written, which treats the reader's page as identical to the writer's. It could come in only if "the reader" became a parameter that includes when they read.
- **The checklist misfires on a recapitulation.** Applied mechanically, "the recap", "the colon reveal" and "bold on a keyword" mark three of this paragraph's best moves as unearned. The model itself (a raise paid against expectation; terms raised in the body) explains why they work.
  - This is within the model. The Unearned-weight list needs one line saying each tell is judged against the ledger, not by its shape.
  - Analyzing an excerpt also needs a way to mark raises as off-page rather than missing.
