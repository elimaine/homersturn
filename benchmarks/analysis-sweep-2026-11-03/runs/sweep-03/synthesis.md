# Candidate additions, ranked by how much the skill would gain

Sets: **G** = good writing (6), **M** = machine prose (6), **S** = written with the skill (4).

**1. Voices other than the writer's**
What: narration in a character's or crowd's words, and the distance between narrator and character; the skill assumes one speaker.
Raised: 5 analyses. G: lady, pride, mississippi. M: storm. S: lighthouse.
Model: inside. A borrowed phrase is a manifold with two readings, the character's and the narrator's. A character's misplaced stress is a raise the writer pays on the next line.
Example: "for when he comes."
Evidence: good; machine (as a failure, the jump to a maxim); skill (works).

**2. The purpose table should classify by reader, and it is missing rows**
What: the table sorts by form, so whole genres misfile or have no row: advice, vignette, opening of a long work, message to someone owed nothing, handles in agent replies, perception in docs.
Raised: 6 analyses. G: walden. M: blog, letter, nature. S: agent-reply, repo-doc.
Model: inside. What is owed depends on who reads and whether they can leave. The vignette row is partly outside, because what it owes is exactness.
Example: "Choose two or three habits that feel meaningful… and adjust as needed."
Evidence: good, machine, skill.

**3. Honest joins beyond therefore/but**
What: "because", "and closer", iterative "and", and procedural "then" are honest joins outside story beats.
Raised: 4 analyses. G: bleak, lady. M: bees. S: naming.
Model: inside. A join is honest if it pays or advances the prior raise, forward or backward.
Example: "A name is a promise."
Evidence: good, machine, skill.

**4. Judge each tell against the ledger, not by its shape**
What: superlatives, colon reveals, recaps, rhetorical questions and adverbs can be load-bearing; check forward before firing.
Raised: 5 analyses. G: lady, mississippi, origin, pride, walden.
Model: inside. The model already says weight is earned by a later payment; only the checklist ignores this.
Example: "There never was so wonderful a book written by man."
Evidence: good only.

**5. The joiner beyond the dash**
What: comma-and, the semicolon, and absolute or participial tails do the dash's joining job and produce the same hum.
Raised: 3 analyses. M: letter, nature. S: agent-reply.
Model: inside. It is the same fault in a different mark, and "and then" one scale down.
Example: "The payments table is migrated; every row crossed but two."
Evidence: machine, skill.

**6. Form that enacts its subject**
What: rhythm, syntax or page shape carries a character's state, the scene's clock, or the claim itself, not only the reader's feeling.
Raised: 5 analyses. G: lady, origin, walden. M: storm. S: naming.
Model: inside if added, as an extension of "rhythm is emotion" and the mindset dial.
Example: "Forty-one. Forty-two."
Evidence: good, machine, skill.

**7. Cliché and stock detail**
What: images and examples the reader completes before reading them, which pay nothing.
Raised: 3 analyses. M: cities, nature, blog.
Model: inside if added. A cliché is a refrain planted by other texts; its sense never shifts.
Example: "an old man feeding pigeons"
Evidence: machine only.

**8. Address and stance toward the reader**
What: "us" versus "you", register owed to a relationship, a text that sorts or casts its reader.
Raised: 3 analyses. G: walden. M: blog, letter.
Model: inside if added. A register mismatch is an accidental reading ("we are strangers now"). Releasing a reader renegotiates the debt.
Example: "With warm regards and sincere apologies"
Evidence: good, machine.

**9. The reader's off-page ledger**
What: raises and payments that live outside the page: allusion, shared history, the reader's memory, inferred payment.
Raised: 4 analyses. G: origin. M: letter. S: naming, lighthouse.
Model: split. As a column with a `[pay: inferred]` mark, it is inside. Its size cannot be read from the page, so that part is outside.
Example: "breathed by the Creator into a few forms or into one"
Evidence: good, machine, skill.

**10. Sound**
What: alliteration, rhyme, stress clusters, chiasmus; how the words sound.
Raised: 5 analyses. G: bleak, mississippi. M: cities. S: agent-reply, lighthouse.
Model: split. Three analyses call it outside, a fourth axis ("what the page sounds like"). Two call it inside, as rhythm at syllable or phrase scale.
Example: "a clean-stemmed dead tree"
Evidence: good, machine, skill.

**11. Accidental readings beyond the sentence**
What: a wrong reading made by adding two clean sentences, or by the scene's physical world.
Raised: 2 analyses. S: repo-doc, lighthouse.
Model: inside if added. It is still where meaning goes, and every number must agree with every rule.
Example: "One click rolls the draft back to the last save"
Evidence: skill only.

**12. The common word with a claimed sense**
What: a plain word that a domain or other software has already loaded with behaviour.
Raised: 2 analyses. S: agent-reply, repo-doc.
Model: inside. It is a manifold whose dominant reading is supplied from off the page.
Example: "To step back, use the undo button."
Evidence: skill only. The skill's own rule pushes toward the failure.

**13. Specificity and exactness**
What: named places and concrete images pay; abstractions and "a city" raise a picture never delivered; exact sensory lines work with no debt.
Raised: 4 analyses. M: cities, blog, letter, nature.
Model: split. Concrete detail as payment is inside if added. Exactness for its own sake is outside.
Example: "the color of strong tea"
Evidence: machine only (both the lack and the working line).

**14. Comedy and irony**
What: comic timing, bathos, deadpan, and a plain reading the voice disowns; amusement is missing from the rhythm list.
Raised: 4 analyses. G: bleak, pride, walden. S: naming.
Model: mostly inside if added. Irony is a raise paid by a reversal within reach; bathos is stress position. Bleak House puts the pleasure itself outside the ledger.
Example: "A feature gets called off-brand for a brand that is three weeks old."
Evidence: good, skill.

**15. A trust column**
What: credibility filled by citations, names, precise numbers and correct grammar, and drawn down by hedges, register lapses and absolute claims.
Raised: 4 analyses. G: lady. M: bees. S: agent-reply, repo-doc.
Model: partly inside, since "concede before you are forced" already treats trust as currency. Agent-reply puts the writer's credibility outside.
Example: "Researchers such as Thomas Seeley have shown"
Evidence: good, machine, skill.

**16. Whether a claim is true**
What: false or contested payments score the same as true ones.
Raised: 4 analyses. M: blog, bees. S: repo-doc (two items).
Model: outside. The ledger is arithmetic over placement, not over fact.
Example: "Psychologists call this reducing decision fatigue."
Evidence: machine, skill.

**17. Tempo: frequency and grammatical time**
What: finite verbs move time and fragments stop it; repetition at scene size can stand for how often something happened.
Raised: 2 analyses. G: bleak. S: lighthouse. Lady's iterative "and" is related.
Model: inside, under Tempo.
Example: "The lamp went round"
Evidence: good, skill.

**18. The extended field**
What: a reading assembled from vocabulary across the piece, never raised in one line.
Raised: 2 analyses. M: bees. S: agent-reply. The Mississippi conceit is related.
Model: inside. It is the manifold at piece scale, and it needs a rule for where it must stop.
Example: "waiting to be claimed"
Evidence: machine, skill (as a risk).

**19. The word-scale motif**
What: the same word turned at close range, or recurring below refrain level.
Raised: 2 analyses. G: walden. S: lighthouse.
Model: inside. It is the refrain at the scale of a word, or a manifold paid within the sentence.
Example: "impertinent… pertinent"
Evidence: good, skill.

**20. Renaming as payment**
What: rename when the reader's knowledge changes or the image tightens.
Raised: 2 analyses. G: lady, mississippi.
Model: inside. Each new name moves the ledger.
Example: "the lady with the dog"
Evidence: good only.

**21. The rare word as carrier**
What: the second meaning lives only in the uncommon word.
Raised: 2 analyses. G: mississippi, walden.
Model: inside. It needs a carve-out from the rare-word rule.
Example: "a sojourner in civilized life"
Evidence: good only.

**22. Punctuation as rest and aside**
What: the parenthesis as sotto voce and the comma as rest, alongside the dash and the swear.
Raised: 2 analyses. G: bleak, origin.
Model: inside, under Accents.
Example: "(if this day ever broke)"
Evidence: good only.

**23. The reader as a parameter (time and knowledge)**
What: period words and lost context; the model assumes the reader shares the writer's world.
Raised: 4 analyses. G: lady, origin, pride, walden.
Model: outside or partly outside. The writer cannot fix it; it belongs to a gloss.
Example: "a chaise and four"
Evidence: good only. Low gain for writing; matters for analysis.

**24. Crescendo**
What: evaluative words climbing by degrees across a paragraph.
Raised: 1 analysis. G: origin. Bleak House's escalation is related.
Model: inside if added. It is placement at the scale of word choice.
Example: "most beautiful and most wonderful"
Evidence: good only.

**25. Convention exemption**
What: a page convention the reader already knows carries no emphasis.
Raised: 1 analysis. G: pride.
Model: inside, under what the page looks like.
Example: 13 of 17 paragraphs are one line.
Evidence: good only.

**26. The borrowed genre signal**
What: another genre's opening move used to set an expectation and then break it.
Raised: 1 analysis. G: bleak.
Model: inside if added to Purpose.
Example: "London."
Evidence: good only.

**27. Deliberate grammatical strain**
What: a construction bent on purpose to carry the theme.
Raised: 1 analysis. M: storm.
Model: inside, under Figures.
Example: "the light was the only thing you could do"
Evidence: machine.

**28. A referent completed by preoccupation**
What: a pronoun resolved from the character's mind, not from syntax.
Raised: 1 analysis. M: storm.
Model: inside. The manifold completes it from the ledger.
Example: "the way he'd shown her."
Evidence: machine.

Single mentions outside the model:

# What the analyses said the skill got wrong

- "One thing, one name" closes renaming as approach and the conceit (lady, mississippi).
- The therefore/but rule marks "because", escalation, iteration and procedure as "and then" (bleak, lady, bees, naming).
- The unearned-weight list fires on shape: colon reveal, recap, bold keyword (lady, origin).
- It also fires on the superlative and the rhetorical question (mississippi, pride).
- It misreads "Unfortunately" as a propping adverb and "natural and pertinent" as a filler doublet (walden).
- "Do not end on atmosphere/setting" contradicts the manifold principle (mississippi; pride ¶11).
- The recursive paragraph is placed only at the end, but it works as an overture (bleak).
- The rhythm table maps awe to "few words and long gaps", but Darwin carries awe in long sentences (origin).
- The proposed S1 fix ("Contemplate a tangled bank") removes the crescendo's low first step (origin).
- "Do not switch rhythm inside an engaged scene" flags rhythm used as a clock (storm).
- "One-line paragraphs stop working at three" flags dialogue turn-taking (pride).
- "Do not refrain a line that was told once" flags "distant land" (walden).
- The rare-word rule says the second meaning falls off; "sojourner" shows the reverse (walden).
- "The common word for the common thing" would swap out "valuable acquisition" and lose the reading (mississippi).
- The same rule pushes toward "undo" and domain-claimed words, whose borrowed sense is wrong (repo-doc, agent-reply).
- The purpose table files an estranged friend as a reader with "a reason to finish" (letter).
- The scene row demands promises, so the vignette fails by definition (nature).
- The ledger would cut "the color of strong tea" as dense on a beat that owes nothing (nature).
- The withheld-word rule and the script's "raised, never paid" contradict dial 5, "more found than told" (lighthouse).
- The dash rule passes a letter that has the joining problem the rule exists for (letter).
- A 318-word block can only be read as a wall, not as stance (walden).
- In an excerpt, the ledger marks off-page raises as missing (origin).
- Proposed change 2 breaks the round/sound rhyme, a cost the skill cannot see (lighthouse).
