Those two came over with no customer behind them, names that point at nobody I can find. I didn't drop them and I didn't guess who they meant. They're parked in a holding table now, waiting to be claimed, held there until you say which way they go.

The invoices I left untouched. Nothing of mine reached them; no row moved, no column changed. Whatever they carried before, they carry still, and not one line is settled.

So holding keeps the two, payments keeps the rest, invoices keep themselves. Tell me to claim the pair or let them go, and I'll finish it.
