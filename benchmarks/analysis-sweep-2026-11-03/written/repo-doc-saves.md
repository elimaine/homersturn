## Piece

# Saving and undo

The editor saves your draft for you. You never press a save button, so you do not lose work by forgetting to.

A save runs on two triggers. The first is a pause: two seconds after you stop typing, the editor writes the current draft to a save. The second is leaving: when you close the page or navigate away from it, the editor writes a save before the page goes. So a draft you are working on stays current within two seconds of your last keystroke, and a draft you walk away from is kept exactly as you left it.

To step back, use the undo button. One click rolls the draft back to the last save, returning you to the state at your most recent pause or exit.

Hold the undo button down instead of clicking it, and the editor shows a list of saves. Each entry is one save, in order, with the newest at the top. Click an entry to restore the draft to that save.

If you close the page by accident, reopen it and the draft is there, as of the save made when you left.
