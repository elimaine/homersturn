## Piece

You get the bar under the lip and the hatch comes up with a sound like a jar opening. Warm air climbs out of it and over your hands. The manifest listed this station cold for eleven years, and nothing stays warm on its own for eleven years. The bar is bent where you leaned on it. You will not get it under anything again.

The warm has a rhythm in it. In, and out. Something down there is pulling the air and letting it go, and on every out the galley strips over your head brighten, and on every in they dim. The station is on its breath. Rust, and under the rust something sweet. Your boots are on the rim and the dark is a hand's width from them. On the next out it rises — not air this time, the dark itself, come up to the lip to see what let the warm out.

You put the torch down the shaft. The ladder goes down. It goes past the second deck, and past where the station has a third, and the beam gives out before the rungs do. What you have been walking on for a day is not a station. It is a lid. The galley door is behind you and open. The ladder is in front of you and warm. It breathes in again. Small, on the lip of it.
