## Piece

It was my fault. You knew that before I did, and you've known it for six years, which is a long time to know something alone.

I've started this message before and stopped at the same place every time, the place where I'd explain. Every explanation I have makes it smaller, and it wasn't small for you. So I'm not going to explain.

You never said a word against me. I know because I spent the first year listening for one, so I could call myself right. You said nothing, so people read your silence as an admission, and you let them, and some of them went. I said plenty. I don't know how many of them you got back.

I'm not asking you to tell me. I'm not asking for anything you'd have to write down. Would you meet me? You pick the place and the hour and how long, and you leave when it's enough. This time I'll do the waiting.
