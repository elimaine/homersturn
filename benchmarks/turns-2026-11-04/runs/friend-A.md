## Piece

It's Mara. I don't know if this is still your number. I was wrong, six years ago, and I have known it the whole time. I'm writing to ask whether you'd meet me, and for nothing else.

I've started this more times than I'll say. Every version was about me. How sorry I am, how much I've thought about it, how I'm different now. You'd have read two lines and put the phone down, and you'd have been right.

What I kept leaving out was you. You stopped coming to Jo's on Thursdays because I'd be there. You missed her wedding because I'd be there. You gave up a room full of people rather than sit across from one, and I let you, for six years, and I kept my seat.

I've given the seat up. Jo knows why, and the rest will if they ask. I'm not telling you so you'll come. I'm telling you so you can go back without wondering. Either way, the room is yours.
